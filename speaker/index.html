Why invite this speaker

From specification
to daily habit

"I don't present concepts. I present systems that thousands of people used this morning without thinking about them — and the decisions that made that possible."

Every talk is grounded in delivered, operating infrastructure: a national ITS platform running since 2018, a nationwide passenger information network, open-loop EMV ticketing, and a cross-system wallet bridge between municipal parking and national transit — each one taken from first study to live operation.

The perspective is rare in conference programmes: not the vendor's, not the academic's, but the facilitator's — the person accountable for the system actually working, every day, in public.
